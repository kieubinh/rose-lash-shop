What to know before your appointment

Brow wax and tint

The Brow Wax + Normal Tint treatment is $25 for 20 minutes, combining brow shaping and colour. The studio guidance says the tint colour typically lasts around one week.

Henna brow tint

Brow Wax + Henna Tint is $40 for 30 minutes, with studio guidance indicating colour may last around two weeks.

Brow lamination package

The $50 package takes approximately 45 minutes and includes waxing, shaping, lamination and tint. The studio recommends leaving around three to four weeks before repeating lamination.
