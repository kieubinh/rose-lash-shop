What to know before your appointment

Face waxing

Face waxing options include Brow Wax ($15), Upper Lip Wax ($10) and Full Face Wax ($40). The full-face service description covers brows, lip, chin and sides.

Body waxing

Rose lists hand, arm, leg and bikini waxing options. Choose from full or half leg, full or half hand, bikini and the listed arm wax and mask service.

Full body waxing package

Full Body Wax is listed at $130, with a duration of around 45–60 minutes and a stated package saving of $25. Confirm included treatment areas directly with Rose before booking.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a leg wax?

Leg Wax is $45 for approximately 15 minutes. Half Leg Wax is $35 for approximately 10 minutes.

Is a full body waxing package available?

Yes. Full Body Wax is listed at $130. Ask Rose to confirm which areas are included.

Can I book online?

You can send an appointment enquiry online; it is not confirmed until the salon replies.
