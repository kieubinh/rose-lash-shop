What to know before your appointment

Finding your lash look

Classic lashes offer a natural everyday finish. Hybrid lashes blend definition and fullness, while volume, wet look and wispy sets create different textures. Mega Volume and Luxury Design are options for a more dramatic result. Your preferred look can be described in the booking enquiry.

Lash infills and maintenance

Two-week infill prices are listed for eligible sets. After approximately three to four weeks, a new set may be needed. Natural Design and Luxury Design are new-set services only.

Lash lift and lash care

The Lash Lift + Tint + Keratin treatment is listed at $50 for 30–45 minutes. The supplied studio guidance indicates the lifted look can last around four to six weeks; discuss when to repeat a treatment with Rose.

Fix policy

Rose offers complimentary fixes for lash problems within three days of the appointment. After three days, additional fees may apply depending on the condition.

Frequently asked questions

How much are classic lash extensions?

A Classic Lashes new set is $70 for 1 hr 15 min. An eligible two-week infill is $50.

Do you offer wispy or mega volume lashes?

Yes. Wispy Lashes are $90 for 1 hr 45 min, and Mega Volume Lashes are $110 for 2 hr.

Is an appointment booked immediately?

No. The online form sends an appointment enquiry; Rose confirms availability and booking details afterwards.
